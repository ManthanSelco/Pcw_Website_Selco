Selco Foundation (“Selco,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) has developed a Platform for Collective Wisdom (“Platform” or “PCW”), a shared digital infrastructure offered by us. The Platform has been developed by Selco on Apurva.AI’s digital infrastructure and is maintained in association with a software service provider.

This Platform, in tandem with other channels of interactions with Selco, including AI-powered tools and other offerings available on our website _____ (collectively, "Interface"), helps users access development-sector knowledge contributed by practitioners and organisations across the country.

The Interface enables users to raise queries and quickly get relevant information for complex decision-making. This initiative aims to leverage a scalable learning approach in the development sector, making it easier to include diverse opinions and make decisions faster.

The automated responses are drawn from structured and unstructured knowledge resources contributed to the PCW by various organisations that Selco has partnered with.
