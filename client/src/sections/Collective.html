Be Part of the Collective

PCW brings together three knowledge perspectives that are often held separately, allowing them to be seen, interpreted, and learned from together.

Voices from Ground

Raw, first-hand accounts from the people programs are designed for, such as farmers, SHG members, health workers, rural entrepreneurs and more.

These are unfiltered conversations captured as shared, reflecting lived realities, everyday decisions, and experiences often missing from formal reports.

Practitioner Perspectives

Insights from people who work closely with communities — program teams, field staff, and practitioners in development. This includes reflections, observations, and learnings from implementing programs on the ground. These perspectives help bridge lived experience with practice, highlighting what works, what doesn’t, and why.

Ecosystem Frameworks

Organisational, research, and programmatic knowledge that situates work within the broader development ecosystem.
This includes strategies, research, evaluations, program frameworks, and sector-wide learnings.Together, these assets provide context, structure, and shared language to interpret ground realities at scale.
