What is the PCW?

Knowledge loses its meaning when it’s siloed.
When lived experiences, field realities, and institutional memory exist in isolation and remain out of reach, we miss the chance to learn better.

PCW weaves the threads.
We are a collaborative, AI-supported platform that brings scattered threads of knowledge together into actionable insights for problem-solving in the development sector.By bridging silos, we make knowledge easy to find, use, and build upon.

A digital and physical space that amplifies grassroots voices, making knowledge accessible, inclusive, and human-first.

Partnerships across communities, practitioners, funders, and policy-makers that turn knowledge into a shared public good.

Actionable insights rooted in lived experience, connecting diverse knowledge threads to address complex, shared challenges
